I'm an open source program and operations professional, I work at the intersection of open source, programs and operations management helping projects and the people behind them build sustainable and lasting impact. Currently, I'm building `Fosstree`, an initiative focused on making open source projects and communities more sustainable and accessible. My goal is to help maintainers, contributors, and organizations create long-term value for the open-source ecosystem.

Previously, I worked with [Open Source Collective](https://oscollective.org/), I was part of the operations team, contributed to support nearly 3000+ of open source projects through financial operations, support, grant administration and other capacities. Over the years, I helped manage and administer more than $12 million in grants and funding programs for open source projects. As a seasoned program manager, I have led many programs for startups and nonprofits. I also worked at [OpenMined](https://openmined.org/), where I led the Private AI mentorship program at OpenMined that launched the [Private AI Series](https://courses.openmined.org/) in collaboration with PyTorch, Facebook AI, Future of Humanity Institute (University of Oxford), and the United Nations Global Working Group on Big Data.

Alongside my professional work, I'm a part-time Psychology student at JSPC, where I'm exploring topics related to brain and behaviour, congnition, environmental psychology and decision making. I also enjoy learning about AI, security and how technology shapes communities.
